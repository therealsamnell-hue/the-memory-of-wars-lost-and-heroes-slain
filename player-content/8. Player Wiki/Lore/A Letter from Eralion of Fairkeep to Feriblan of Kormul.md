---
title: A Letter from Eralion of Fairkeep to Feriblan of Kormul
publish: true
---
There is a truth I must set down ere my hand grows cold to mortal ink. When last we shared wine and argument by your fire, I did not speak plainly. I wrapped honesty in scholar’s caution and let you believe my questions idle curiosities. Forgive me, for that was unworthy of our long friendship.

You will recall our talk — long into the night, while the candles guttered low — of liches, of their grim endurance, and of the perilous roads by which a mage might pass from flesh into deathless will. I laughed then, as though the matter were no more than speculation. Yet my interest was never merely academic. I fear you knew it, even as you kindly let me pretend.

You, more than any soul alive, know what it is to peer into darkness for the sake of knowledge. You have done so and returned with wisdom enough to make lesser folk whisper of madness. I have followed that path as well, though farther than I once believed possible. Through bargains best left unwritten and struggles that still stain my dreams, I have wrested from the dread Prince of Undeath, Orcus, the secret that so many seek and so few survive to name. I will not be content with theory any longer. I mean to claim lichdom for myself.

Think on it, my friend — years uncounted stretching ahead like a road lit by ever-burning lamps. No failing body, no trembling hand, no creeping forgetfulness to steal the Art from me. Only study. Mastery. Power grown patient and absolute.

As I write, my chambers are transformed into a sanctum of preparation. Brass bowls of unguents scent the air with sharp spice and grave-dust sweetness; crystal vials glow faintly with trapped spells; knives of silver, obsidian, and stranger substances rest beside ancient folios whose pages whisper when turned. All is prepared save the final necessity — a phylactery crafted to exact design. The last ingredients lie within your city’s walls, and I go now to claim them. By the time this letter reaches your hands, I shall already be riding for my keep, where the final rite awaits.

Yet in these last hours of mortality, I find my thoughts straying to you. Enclosed with this letter is a small pouch of blue silk. Within lies an amulet, wrought by my own hand and bound to my wards. Wear it, and the doors of my keep shall open to you as to a guest long expected. Should your thirst for knowledge grow unbearable — as I know it must — you may come to me, and I will share what I have learned beyond the threshold of death.

Others have long called you mad for the questions you dare ask. Soon, perhaps, they will call me the same. Let them mutter in taprooms and behind closed shutters. Their words matter little beside what I am about to become.

I go now to seize immortality, and with it the freedom to pursue the Art without end. When next we meet, it may be by candlelight untouched by time itself.

Come, when you are ready. Come gaze into the darkness once more — and see what answers it gives.

Ever your friend, in life and beyond it,

Eralion of Fairkeep
